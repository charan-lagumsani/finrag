Under Regulation NMS and FINRA Rule 4511, member firms must maintain books and
records that accurately reflect all transactions. Trade reports must be
submitted to the appropriate Trade Reporting Facility within 10 seconds of
execution for most equity transactions.

Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI) requires broker-dealers to act in the best
interest of retail customers when making a recommendation. Firms must
establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably
designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI, including the Disclosure,
Care, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance obligations.

Failure to supervise automated trading systems has resulted in enforcement
actions where firms lacked pre-trade risk controls. Examiners increasingly
request documentation of model validation for AI-driven surveillance tools,
including evidence of hallucination testing for generative AI summaries used
in suspicious activity reviews.
